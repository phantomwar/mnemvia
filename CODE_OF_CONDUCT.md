# Community participation

Mnemvia welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Be respectful, explain technical disagreements and make room for questions and corrections.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate publication of private information and persistent personal attacks are not acceptable. Criticism should address the proposal or behavior, not demean the person.

These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, discussions and spaces operated by the project. Maintainers may edit or remove abusive material, warn participants, limit access or exclude repeat or severe offenders. Decisions should be proportionate and documented without exposing private reports.

For a sensitive conduct concern, contact an identified maintainer privately through a verified contact once the project publishes its maintainer roster. If the concern involves that maintainer, use another listed maintainer or the hosting platform's reporting process. Do not post private allegations or personal information in a public issue.

Current limitation: this design-stage repository has no published maintainer roster or dedicated private conduct contact. Establish both before opening community discussion channels. No staffed enforcement team or response SLA is implied.
